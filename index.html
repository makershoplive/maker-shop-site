Questions

Good to know.

What do I pay each month?

Nothing to me and nothing for hosting. PayPal takes its normal fee when you make a sale. If you want your own web address like yourshop.com, that's about $15 a year.

Do I need a PayPal Business account?

Yes. Upgrading a personal PayPal account to Business is free. Your customers don't need PayPal; they can pay with any debit or credit card.

Can I add products myself after it's built?

Yes, that's the point. Add, edit, mark sold and restock from your phone whenever you want. You never need me to add a product.

What if I want changes later?

Your shop text, shipping and products are always yours to change. For design changes or new features after launch, I charge a simple hourly rate.

What kinds of shops is this for?

Makers and small sellers with a handful to a few dozen products: jewelry, candles, art, crafts, baked goods that ship, and so on. If you need hundreds of products or complicated inventory, a bigger platform is a better fit.

What about sales tax?

The shop doesn't add sales tax at checkout. That's common for small makers, but check your own state's rules.
